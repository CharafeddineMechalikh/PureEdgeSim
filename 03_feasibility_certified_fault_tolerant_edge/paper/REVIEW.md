## Round 1 — major revision

**R3.1.** Several result tables were typed by hand into the manuscript and
disagreed with the artifact (e.g. an EDGE_AND_CLOUD success of 0.71 where the
data say 0.121; a stage delay of 0.036 where the data say 0.639).
*Resolution.* All result tables are now `\input` of `results/tables/*.tex`,
generated by the analysis script. The hand-typed tables were deleted, and the
manuscript was rebuilt around the generated ones. A scanning rule was adopted:
no number in a table is transcribed.

**R3.2.** The findings were asserted without statistical support.
*Resolution.* Added a paired comparison table with Mann–Whitney U, Holm
correction, bootstrap CIs and Cliff's δ; the text now reports δ = −1.00 for the
deadline-failure reduction and non-significance for the makespan change.

**R3.3.** The certificate's soundness is asserted, not proved.
*Resolution.* Added Proposition 1 (soundness under a Gaussian error model) with
a proof, Proposition 2 (monotone conservatism in z and m) with a proof, and
Proposition 3 (retry accounting) with a proof. The proofs are short but complete,
and the assumptions are stated explicitly.

**R3.4.** The claim "precision 1.000" needs a counterfactual.
*Resolution.* Added the shadow-mode audit design and a 16-point operating
surface. Precision and false-admission rate are measured by letting the vetoed
task run.

**R3.5.** A single operating point cannot justify the default (z = 1.282,
margin = 0.02).
*Resolution.* Added the full 4 × 4 surface, two heatmaps and a precision–recall
plot; the text identifies the over-conservative corner (recall 0.584 at
z = 1.96, margin 0.20).
