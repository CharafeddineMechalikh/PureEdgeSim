-- Simulator patch: battery energy accounting
--
-- File:        _deps_pureedgesim_python/src/pureedgesim/topology.py
--              _deps_pureedgesim_python/src/pureedgesim/nodes.py
-- Applies to:  the vendored PureEdgeSim tree used by every grid in this folder.
--
-- DEFECT
-- ------
-- Topology creation preloaded the *unused* share of the battery into the
-- consumption accumulator so that `capacity - consumption` would yield the
-- remaining charge:
--
--     if spec.battery:
--         energy.cpu_energy_consumption = energy.battery_capacity * (1 - initial/100)
--
-- Every reported energy total is `cpu_energy_consumption + network_...`, so the
-- same work was reported as costing 228 Wh at 20 % initial charge, 144 Wh at
-- 50 % and 3.4 Wh at 100 %, when no device died and the workload was identical.
-- The remaining-battery figure was correct; the consumption figure was not.
--
-- FIX
-- ---
-- Remove the preload from topology.py, and apply the initial charge where it
-- belongs, in `ComputingNode.get_remaining_battery`:
--
--     available = battery_capacity * initial_battery_level / 100.0
--     return max(0.0, available - total_energy_consumption())
--
-- `get_total_energy_consumption()` now means consumption since the run started.
-- `get_remaining_battery()` is numerically unchanged, so depletion and
-- device-death behaviour is bit-identical.
--
-- VERIFICATION
-- ------------
-- The `energy` grid was re-run (60 runs, results/runs/energy). Energy per task is
-- 0.00724 Wh in all three battery cells (spread exactly 0.0), and the policy
-- comparison is 0.00730 (joint) vs 0.00741 Wh per successful request (naive).
-- Before the patch the three cells differed by two orders of magnitude.
--
-- The other grids were re-run only where they report energy; their default
-- initial battery is 100 %, for which the old offset was zero, so their numbers
-- are unaffected by the patch.
